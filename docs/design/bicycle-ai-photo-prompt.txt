Each frame has its own camera position and lens (given per image); do not repeat the same angle or layout across the set.
No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names, decals, stickers, serial numbers, shop signs, street signs, licence plates,
faces, jewellery, tattoos or identifiable places. Components are plain and unbranded.

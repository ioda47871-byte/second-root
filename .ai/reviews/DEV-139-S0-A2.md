## Verdict: FAIL (Critical 0 / High 1 / Medium 1 / Low 4)

What the PR does is sound mechanically. C1-C7 pass, the six pins reproduce, the copied facts packs match their sources byte for byte, and nothing protected is touched. It fails because the six pins are meant to go to the owner as the approval request for real generation. Four of them (bakery and café) carry prompt rules written for the wagashi shop that contradict those shops' facts, and the doc does not say so.

## Findings

### H1: 4 of 6 pinned prompts tell the model to show a wrong address and to "evoke the sweets" (not disclosed)
- Where: `lib/design-agent/phase2f/prompt.ts:96` (V2_FACT_RULES: "for the address show only 名古屋市東区") and `:101` (V2_IMAGE_RULES.B: "evoke the sweets"). Both are added to every v2 prompt (`:207`). The pins are recorded in `design-library/research/quality-pipeline/poc/s0-generation-selection.json` (bakery and café entries) and in `docs/operations/s0-three-shop-directions.md` §選んだ6案 / §実生成の承認に持っていくもの (around line 103).
- Proof: I rebuilt the café c1 prompt in scratch with `buildDesignPrompt`. The SHA matched the recorded `89e23f6b…` and the text contains both rules. The bakery facts say 名古屋市昭和区…, and the café facts say 架空市架空区…. Nothing in lib or scripts makes an exception for 名古屋市東区 outside prompt.ts.
- What goes wrong: an approved real-generation run would spend part of the 12-call budget on prompts whose rules conflict with their own facts. Either the model shows 名古屋市東区 for a bakery or café (a false address claim, which the copy or identity gate should then fail), or it is confused by the "sweets" instruction. That contaminates the false-claim and originality measurements the experiment exists to take.
- Fix: in this PR, record this as a blocker in the doc and the selection file, and state that the bakery and café pins cannot go into the approval request as they are. Changing prompt.ts (Phase 2f, isolated and protected) to make these rules shop-generic needs owner approval. Once it changes, the six SHAs change and must be pinned again. Until then, only the two wagashi pins are consistent with their facts.

### M1: the selection reasons contradict each other, and the six leave out the most image-free option
- Where: `s0-generation-selection.json:125` rejects w3 because "B方式 (画像なし) では…比喩が弱まりやすい". Yet bakery d2 is selected even though it is the most image-dependent of all 9: 6 image slots, imagery `collage`, a `full_bleed_media` hero of 石窯の口, and protect "品が格子に揃わず床に置かれていること". c1 also has a full-bleed image hero. The `d3` rejection (its bakery entry, `why`) says alternating_spreads would overlap `c3`, but c3 is not selected either, so the reason does not hold.
- What goes wrong: the six contain no type_led hero and no alternating_spreads flow. On a route that allows no images and forbids imitating photographs, d2's product-on-the-floor collage is likely to collapse into scattered product names on a gradient. Meanwhile d3 (type_led, imagery none, the strongest fit for the B route) sits unused.
- Fix: either swap d2 for d3, or give an honest reason why d2 survives without images and apply the same standard to w3.

### L1: a count in a selection reason is wrong
- `s0-generation-selection.json:88` says c3 and d3 share "8観点中4つ". The descriptors share 3 (flow, navigation, reading_path). Fix the number.

### L2: the SP plans, sections and protect never reach the generation prompt

Both research entries of a direction also get the same `translate_to_this_shop` text (the motif's becomes_structure). The doc table (`s0-three-shop-directions.md:39`) presents SP composition as something that sets the directions apart, but the model never sees `sp_plan`. The B6 SP differences are declarations only.
- Fix: say this in the doc, so the C-stage comparison does not expect the declared SP plans to show up.

### L3: soft spots in the direction texts against Identity Lock (mitigated by the "facts verbatim" display rule)
- d2 concept and sections place all five items (F12–F16) on the stone-oven floor and show "熱の残る位置". The facts only say the 田舎パン is stone-oven baked (F9).
- The d2 `heat` section (`bakery/direction-set.json:318`) is a temperature legend, which invites invented temperatures.

- w3 (not selected; `wagashi/direction-set.json:373`) says "店頭の台に一つだけ置かれる" (invented).

### L4: "9 different hero x flow pairs" comes from rotating a small vocabulary
- The 9 directions use 4 heroes (diagram/scene/object/type) and 4 flows. Each shop's set is close to a permutation of the same menu, and the uniqueness test (`three-shop-directions.test.ts`, "nine different hero × flow pairs") cannot detect a re-skin.
- The motifs themselves are shop-specific: kiln clock, hearth floor, window street, nel drop, wrapping sheet, tsubu. The café is not a re-skin of the bakery.
- Do not present the pair test as evidence of originality.

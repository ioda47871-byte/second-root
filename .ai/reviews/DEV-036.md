# DEV-036 review

## Cause

- Two Staging PoC jobs (a baked_goods demo and a bakery demo) ended with RENDERER_CHANGE_NEEDED. Both final profiles already used `spacing: tight`, a `centered_cover` / `medium` hero and no motifs, so no profile change could make them more compact.
  - The review still wanted less mobile whitespace (section padding and heading gaps 25–30 % smaller), English section headings about 20 % smaller, a less padded medium centered cover, and a smaller short name on desktop.
- In the CSS, each section's `padding-top` and `.section + .section` `margin-top` both used `--gap`, so the space between sections was twice the intended rhythm. `.main` then added one more `--gap` before the footer.
- Tight phones kept the 2.2 rem heading minimum, the 24 px heading gap, and the medium hero's 40svh height and 36 px title padding. On desktop, the short name was capped only at 13 rem (208 px).

## Change (`components/demo/profile/profile.module.css` only)

- Phones (< 720 px) under `spacing: tight`:
  - `--gap` is 32 px;
  - sections have no extra margin (one rhythm: padding plus the divider);
  - the heading gap is 16 px;
  - the footer gap is 24 px;
  - headings are `clamp(1.75rem, 7vw, 2.6rem)`.
- Phones, `centered_cover` + `medium` (for every spacing): min-height is 28svh, the title padding is 20 px, and the hero's bottom padding is 20 px.
- Desktop (≥ 720 px):
  - In `centered_cover` + `medium`, the name is capped at 9 rem (both name variants). Long names still shrink to fit the width.
  - Under tight, sections are 24 px apart (plus their padding) and the footer gap is 32 px.
- Unchanged:
  - enums, the schema, review scoring and thresholds, `needs_renderer_change`, max revisions, and the brief threshold;
  - the name tokenizer and fitting (DEV-034), and the other layouts, heights and spacings.

## Observation (no policy change)

- In both PoC jobs, the rejected low-confidence briefs (0.43 and 0.46) pointed at the directions the runs finally settled on (`boutique_minimal` and `japanese_modern`). The 0.5 threshold may cost one revision to rediscover a useful brief.
- The threshold stays at 0.5. It will be re-evaluated after the controlled rerun with this renderer.

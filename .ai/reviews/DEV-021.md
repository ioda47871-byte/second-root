## Round 3: PASS (Critical 0 / High 0 / Medium 1 / Low 4)

All round-2 fixes are verified with about 120 probes. The probes also showed no false positives on ordinary replies: times, dates, decimals, amounts, LINEでも, ZWJ emoji, No.1 and Ver.2.

| Sev | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | The demo URL boundary was a deny-list, so `DEMO;/../other`, `DEMO)/../`, `DEMO\..\` and similar opened another shop's demo | Fixed. The URL now counts only when followed by an allow-listed character: end of text, whitespace, Japanese closing punctuation, or `. ! ? )` followed by end or space. It is removed before full stops are canonicalized. Any leftover `..` or `/.` is refused. Tests added. |
| Low | ASCII periods between words were refused as domains (`Next.js`, `ありがとう．よろしく`) | The prompt now tells the drafter to use 「。」 and no `.`/`@` in sentences. Failed drafts are retried, and back-off prevents blocking the queue. |
| Low | `LINEのIDは` and `ラインID` were not caught | Fixed: `(LINE\|ライン)…(ID\|@\|アカウント)`. Tests added. |
| Low | Refusal false positives (`ご遠慮なく`, `迷惑ではない`, `結構嬉しい`) | Fixed: these polite phrases are excluded before detection. Tests added. |
| Low | IDN whose TLD contains ー (`焼き菓子.クッキー`) | Fixed: the IDN check now runs on visible text and includes ー. Test added. |

## Findings and responses

1. **High — the fix may not address the CI mechanism.**
   - **What the reviewer found:** Playwright's `page.close()` already sends `Target.closeTarget` at once. The CI logs (3 runs) show the page stopped only after DEV-041's *second* close, about 10 s later. The likelier cause is that Chromium drops a close that arrives during the cross-host navigation; a stalled `page.close()` is less likely.
   - **What was wrong with f6613d2:** with `now`, `closeTargetByCdp` sent its single close in that same window and only polled after that. With a dropped close it would run for the full `ms` and end in `CLOSE_TIMEOUT`, which is worse than develop.
   - CI on f6613d2 confirmed this: `C CLOSE_TIMEOUT` in the new test, and an existing DEV-041 test with stuck pages still listed.
   - **Fixed:**
     - `closeTargetByCdp` sends `Target.closeTarget` (same exact id) again on every 100 ms poll until Chromium no longer lists the target, or the deadline passes. This applies to both the `now` path and the old fallback.
     - With `now`, the targeted close is also retried until the deadline. Right after the page opened, its id may not be remembered yet (`rememberTargetId` is bounded), and reading it can fail while the page moves.
2. **Medium — the new tests only modelled "page.close() never sends anything".**
   - **Fixed:** a new test, "a targeted close that Chromium drops is sent again until the page is gone", swallows the first `Target.closeTarget` on the browser CDP session. It asserts `target_closed` within 1.5 s and leaves the other page open.
   - It fails without the resend (checked by temporarily removing the resend line: 2.7 s and fail) and passes with it.

## Checked and fine (reviewer)

- **Concurrent close:** every ordering resolves correctly (closed / target_closed / timeout), within `ms`, with no unhandled rejections. Two `closeTarget` sends are harmless.
- **DEV-035 invariants:** the close is never made from the request handler, each page is closed once, and the run stays bounded. **DEV-041:** the target is matched by exact id, never by URL.
- **When `now` is used:** only in C on an off-site stop. A and B are guarded, a wall uses the ordinary close, and without `now` the old path is byte-identical.
- **Callers:** only the `diagnose` command uses these functions. Capture and the worker are unaffected.
- **The new tests are meaningful:** both fail on develop's `diagnose.ts`.

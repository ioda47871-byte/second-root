## Findings

- **L1: `CATEGORY_KINDS` adds generic attributes that are not in the facts** (`lib/design-agent/phase2f/prompt.ts:126,128`: "bread", "coffee and light food").
  - Failure scenario: a future café pack with only drinks would still be told it serves "light food". Under the image rule ("evoke what this shop makes") the model might draw food the facts never name.
  - Mitigation: the same line says the description and products "say exactly what it makes or serves", and the 6 current packs agree with the glosses.
  - Fix (later, needs owner approval because it changes the v2.1 prompt bytes and its pins): keep only the kind name ("a café (喫茶)"), or mark the gloss as "typically".
- **L2: the test comment overstates its coverage** (`tests/unit/design-agent/phase2f/prompt-unchanged.test.ts:13`).
  - The comment says "every committed facts fixture", but the test pins 5 of 9: bicycle, sendable-tamaki, sento and tsugute are left out.
  - I checked all 9 independently and they are unchanged, so the fix is the wording (the doc's "5つの facts" is accurate) or adding the 4 packs.
- **L3: the design-input record names the wrong version for a v2.1 run** (`lib/sales/b-route/generation-prompt.ts:84,144`).
  - `pin.json` from prepare-design-input always records `prompt_version: "v2"`, including for the planned v2.1 run.
  - from-generation does not compare this field with call.json, so nothing breaks. But the run's records will disagree about the version.
  - Fix: add a note to `s0-first-generation.md` that this field names the open-direction input shape, or make the field version-neutral in a later task.
- Info: `notActivated` asks the parser with route B and no research. tm0, v2 and v2.1 then fall through to their usual checks, which is intended.
- Info: the open L3 from the earlier review (the bakery facts were written alongside its directions) still applies when judging shop-specificity.

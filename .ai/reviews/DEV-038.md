# DEV-038 review

## Cause

- After DEV-036, the controlled rerun still ended with RENDERER_CHANGE_NEEDED. Scores were 15 → 15 → 16 and 16 → 16 → 17. Both runs had a brief at 0.83 with profile source codex (no 0.5 fallback), and both finals were `centered_cover / medium / tight`.
- The last reviews asked for:
  - A: a lower desktop medium hero, a smaller mobile address → access gap, and less footer padding;
  - B: about 25 % less vertical padding on the centered cover, tighter mobile menu rows, and a smaller mobile notice / footer.
- These are renderer constants that DEV-036 did not cover:
  - the desktop medium floor (56svh);
  - `.menuItem` / `.row` padding (16 px) and the `.visit` gap (40 px);
  - DemoFrame's notice (0.8rem, 10 px padding) and footer (28 / 40 px padding, paragraph margins).

## Change (`components/demo/profile/profile.module.css` only)

- **Phones, `tight`:**
  - The canvas's own medium centered cover has a 22svh floor, 14 px top and bottom padding, and 14 px title padding.
  - Menu and information rows have 10 px padding.
  - `.visit` gap is 20 px and `.address` gap is 8 px.
- **Desktop, `tight`:** the canvas's own medium centered cover has a 38svh floor (was 56svh), 18 / 24 px padding and 24 px title padding.
- **Any width, `tight`:** the footer has 16 / 20 px vertical padding. It sits outside the canvas, so the rule is `.root:has(> .canvas[data-spacing="tight"]) > footer`.
- **Phones, every profile demo:** the notice and footer are 0.75rem (12 px), with tighter line-height and padding and no paragraph margins.
  - All wording is kept.
  - The template demos (`demo.module.css`) are unchanged.
- **Unchanged:**
  - DEV-036's section gaps;
  - DEV-034's name fitting;
  - split photo heroes (the rules use `.canvas > .hero`, so a split hero keeps no floor on phones and 56svh on desktop);
  - standard / generous spacing;
  - enums, schema, scoring, thresholds, blocking, max revisions and the brief threshold.

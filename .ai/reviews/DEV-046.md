# DEV-046: brief structure wording (VOCABULARY / RULES only)

Why: `.ai/reviews/DEV-045.md`, causes 1 and 2. The sparse-evidence rule ("choose a calm direction") also steered the structure. Some structural options had no description, and the `split_crop` example assumed a spaced Latin name.

## Change (`lib/design-agent/prompts.ts`, the `VOCABULARY` and `RULES` strings only)

- **`heroLayout.layout`:**
  - Intro: "each one sets the shop name as the hero's main visual".
  - `split_crop`: "one word per line, every other line pushed right" (the example is kept). "Only a Latin name of two to four spaced words gets this split." Any other name (with Japanese in it, without spaces, or longer; most names here) "is set without a word-by-word split, centred vertically in the hero". `setsInPieces` and `nameLines` (`text.ts:314-316`, `36-39`) and `ProfileRenderer.tsx:108` decide this. The text no longer claims "oversized" or "cropped", which hold only for the spaced-Latin split.
  - `stacked_oversized`: "the whole name set at the bottom-left of the hero". "Of a tall hero" and "as large as the width allows" were dropped: height is a separate choice, and every layout uses the same name sizing.
  - `centered_cover`: "the name centred, and the area line centred below a double rule". The old "magazine cover: folio line on top … area line below" credited it with the folio and area line, which every layout has (`ProfileRenderer.tsx:141, 198`).
- **`heroLayout.height`:** a minimum that grows with content.
  - `full`: the whole first screen on desktop, about as tall as `tall` on phones.
  - `tall`: most of the first screen.
  - `medium`: about half the first screen; without a photo, a band with spacing tight. The existing renderer asymmetry is stated, not hidden: "a medium centered_cover is a band on phones at every spacing" (DEV-036, `profile.module.css:380`).
  - The `alignment: left | center (text in the lower sections).` text is byte-identical (now on its own line; separate and inert, DEV-044 notes). Split photo heroes are not described; their desktop floors do not follow the order (renderer quirk, out of scope).
- **`composition.grid`:** all three are described with their ratios (editorial_12 about 1 : 3; asymmetric_7_5 label 5 : text 7; single_column headings above their text). A note says the grid applies from tablet width and phones always show one column (≥ 720 px only).
- **`composition.infoStyle`:** `ledger_columns` is described as it renders: label and value on one row, joined by a dotted leader.
- **`spacing.scale`:** described by what changes: the space between sections (gap 104 / 72 / 48 px). For tight, a medium hero without a photo becomes a band, and on phones the rows and headings are closer too.
- **`RULES`:**
  - New: structure (hero layout and height, grid, info style, spacing) follows the content's shape (the name's length, script and spaces, and how much verified content there is). "Every listed option works on a short page; none of them is a default or a safe choice. Do not choose structure for calmness."
  - The sparse-evidence rule now keeps "calm" for brand styling only (direction, palette, typography, motifs), says "This applies to styling only, not to structure", and keeps "set confidence below 0.5".
- **Unchanged:** schema, enums and their order, the 0.5 threshold, score, `MAX_REVISIONS`, the renderer, `heroLayout.alignment`, divider / motif / motion / typography / palette wording, the photo vocabulary's photo line, and every safety rule.
- **Shared text:** `VOCABULARY` and `RULES` are shared by the brief, the review and the photo prompts. The reviewer therefore also reads the structure wording, which keeps it consistent with the brief. This is part of the experiment and is recorded for attribution.

- Critical 0 / High 0. Scope, snapshots and the tests themselves are clean. Five Medium findings, all factual inaccuracies in the new descriptions, all fixed:
  1. `split_crop` implied spaced Japanese names split by word. Any name with Japanese in it uses the pieces path, and a Latin name of 5+ words is one line.
  2. `full` "the whole first screen" is true only on desktop. On phones `full` is 80svh − 40 against `tall`'s 78svh − 40.
  3. `medium` "a band at the top" is true only with spacing tight. Otherwise it is 40svh on phones and 56svh on desktop.
  4. Spacing "within sections" and "rows closer" overstated it. Spacing changes the gap between sections; rows and headings are closer only on phones under tight.
  5. The height ordering does not hold for a split photo hero (`.heroSplit .hero { min-height: 0 }`). The first fix ("keeps its own height") was itself wrong on desktop; see review 2. The quirk itself is in the renderer and out of scope.
- Lows:
  - fixed: `centered_cover` was still the richest description (L1); the `asymmetric_7_5` "wider" was ambiguous (L2); `single_column` wording (L3); the test helper ran `ledger_columns` into the divider list (L6, now tested).
  - noted, no change:
    - the "how much verified content" wording might nudge sparse pages toward smaller structures (L4; watch it in the rerun);
    - divider and motion are named in neither rule (L5; consistent with the human decision);
    - no direct photo-review-prompt test (L7; the snapshot covers it).
- While fixing, `centered_cover`'s new text was checked against the CSS. The folio is the same three-column bar in every layout, so the text says only that the name and the area line are centred, with the double rule above the area line (`profile.module.css:212, 229-242`).

# DEV-046: brief structure wording (VOCABULARY / RULES only)

Why: `.ai/reviews/DEV-045.md`, causes 1 and 2. The sparse-evidence rule ("choose a calm direction") also steered the structure. Some structural options had no description, and the `split_crop` example assumed a spaced Latin name.

## Change (`lib/design-agent/prompts.ts`, the `VOCABULARY` and `RULES` strings only)

- **`heroLayout.layout`:**
  - Intro: "each one sets the shop name as the hero's main visual".
  - `split_crop` now says a name of two to four spaced words is set one word per line (the example is kept). A name without spaces (most Japanese names) is kept in whole pieces: one line on desktop, as few lines as fit on phones, "so the split shows less". This matches the renderer (`ProfileRenderer.tsx:108`, `text.ts:314`, DEV-043 notes) and does not claim the same split for Japanese names.
  - `stacked_oversized` no longer says "of a tall hero" (height is a separate choice).
  - `centered_cover` is unchanged.
- **`heroLayout.height`:** each value is described (full / tall / medium), plus "the hero grows past it when its content needs more". The `alignment: left | center (text in the lower sections).` text is unchanged (separate, inert; DEV-044 notes).
- **`composition.grid`:** all three are described. A note says the grid applies from tablet width and phones always show one column, which is true (`profile.module.css`, ≥ 720 px only).
- **`composition.infoStyle`:** `ledger_columns` is now described as it renders: label and value on one row, joined by a dotted leader.
- **`spacing.scale`:** all three are described.
- **`RULES`:**
  - New: structure (hero layout and height, grid, info style, spacing) follows the content's shape (the name's length, script and spaces, and how much verified content there is). "Every listed option works on a short page; none of them is a default or a safe choice. Do not choose structure for calmness."
  - The sparse-evidence rule now keeps "calm" for brand styling only (direction, palette, typography, motifs), says "This applies to styling only, not to structure", and keeps "set confidence below 0.5".
- **Unchanged:** schema, enums and their order, the 0.5 threshold, score, `MAX_REVISIONS`, the renderer, `heroLayout.alignment`, divider / motif / motion / typography / palette wording, the photo vocabulary's photo line, and every safety rule.
- **Shared text:** `VOCABULARY` and `RULES` are shared by the brief, the review and the photo prompts. The reviewer therefore also reads the structure wording, which keeps it consistent with the brief. This is part of the experiment and is recorded for attribution.

## Tests

- New `tests/unit/design-agent/brief-structure-wording.test.ts` (6 tests):
  - every value of the hero layout, height, grid, info style and spacing has a description of its own;
  - no structural option's description contains preference words (default, safe, recommend, prefer, best, usual, typical, calm);
  - "calm" appears only in the two rules (the structure rule that forbids it and the styling rule), never in `VOCABULARY`, and the styling rule names no structural option;
  - the `split_crop` wording covers spaced and unspaced names without claiming the same split;
  - the safety, low-confidence, alignment and no-photo texts are unchanged;
  - the brief and the review carry the same text.
- With develop's `prompts.ts`, 4 of the 6 fail. The other 2 are guards: safety texts, and shared text.
- Snapshots (`photo-codex`, `review-scale`) were updated by exact text replacement, not `vitest -u`. Every changed snapshot line comes from the new or old `VOCABULARY` / `RULES` (checked line by line).
- Full unit suite: 907 passed, 16 skipped (environment-gated). typecheck, lint and check:secrets are clean.
